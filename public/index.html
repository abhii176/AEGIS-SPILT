Aegis-Split

Hybrid AI & Human Micro-Escrow Engine

A merged GitHub pull request is checked against the milestone’s acceptance criteria. Objective work is paid in full through PayPal when that check passes. Subjective work can wait for the client to sign off. If they disagree, either person can raise a dispute and a counselor decides from the chat, the audit, and the diff. Platform fee: 0%.

  • Client ghosting Objective milestones pay on merge. The client cannot stall that payout.
  • Scope creep Acceptance criteria are saved when the milestone is created. There is no edit button.
  • Paid per module Each milestone is its own PayPal hold and its own payout.

            What this agreement covers
            

              

                
1. Identity gate

                
Projects, funding, and merges stay blocked until the ID flag and the consent log exist.

              

              

                
2. On-platform chat

                
Project messages stay in the app. The product does not detect WhatsApp, email, or other outside chats.

              

              

                
3. Exit penalty

                
A one-sided client cancel pays 50% of the remaining vault to the freelancer through PayPal. The rest goes back to the client.

              

              

                
4. Conduct

                
The consent agreement forbids abuse and fraud. There is no separate legal-action workflow.
